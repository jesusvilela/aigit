# AIGit brand

## The mark

![AIGit semantic graph mark](../assets/brand/aigit-mark.png)

The mark is a **semantic graph**: three connected nodes retain Git's sense of history and branching, while the central ring represents context around a change. The small vermilion point is the human signal—the moment of review, judgment, or provenance.

It is designed to read as a recognizable silhouette at small sizes: calm structure first, detail second.

## Voice

**Git for files. AIGit for intent.**

Write with short, direct sentences. Lead with what becomes clearer for developers. Keep the tone quiet, precise, and genuinely open about alpha-stage uncertainty.

Use words such as *intent, lineage, context, provenance, reviewable,* and *deterministic*. Avoid hype, generic “AI-powered” phrasing, neon-futurist imagery, and claims that imply AIGit replaces Git.

## Use

- Use the transparent [`aigit-mark.png`](../assets/brand/aigit-mark.png) on light, paper-toned, or very dark solid backgrounds.
- Keep clear space around the mark equal to roughly the width of its central ring.
- Use the mark by itself for avatars and favicons; pair it with the word **AIGit** in an unembellished sans-serif for larger surfaces.
- Preserve the vermilion point. It is the recognisable human accent.

Do not stretch, recolor the full mark, add gradients, place it on busy imagery, or imitate another Git product's iconography.
