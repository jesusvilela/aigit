# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want AIGit to be a calm, rigorous place to build. Participation is expected to be welcoming, constructive, and respectful across experience levels, identities, and viewpoints.

## Expected behavior

- Assume good intent; ask before concluding.
- Critique ideas, designs, and code—not people.
- Share context and make room for different working styles.
- Treat real-world failures and uncertainty as useful evidence.
- Respect privacy, consent, and the security of others’ work.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, or sharing private information without permission are not acceptable. Neither is disruptive behavior that prevents others from participating.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and take other action proportionate to the behavior. To report a concern, open a private security advisory on GitHub or contact the repository owner through the contact information on the project profile. Reports will be handled discreetly and in good faith.
